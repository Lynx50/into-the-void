Refine my app "Into the Void" to strengthen the emotional experience and make users feel a subtle sense of connection after receiving a thought.

GOAL:
Move the app from “nice interface” to “something I’d actually open again”.

Do NOT add new features.
Only improve emotional impact and user experience.

==================================================
1. ADD CONNECTION LINE AFTER REVEAL (MOST IMPORTANT)
==================================================

After a thought is revealed, display a small, subtle line of text underneath the thought.

Examples (randomly rotate between these):
- "You're not alone."
- "Someone else felt this too."
- "Another mind, same thought."
- "This came from someone like you."

Style:
- small font
- low contrast (soft grey/white)
- slightly transparent
- centered
- appears 0.5–1 second after the thought appears

IMPORTANT:
This should feel like a quiet whisper, not a headline.

==================================================
2. ADD LINGER MOMENT (CRITICAL)
==================================================

After the thought appears:

- slightly dim the background
- keep the screen still
- delay showing action buttons by 1–2 seconds

Add:
- very subtle breathing glow around the bubble or text
- soft fade-in for the connection line

Goal:
Encourage user to pause and reflect for a few seconds.

==================================================
3. IMPROVE OPENING EMOTIONAL HOOK
==================================================

Update the home screen text above the receive button.

Replace:
"A thought is waiting for you..."

With one of:
- "Someone left this here..."
- "This came from someone else..."
- "A thought from someone like you..."

Keep tone:
- human
- subtle
- slightly mysterious

==================================================
4. ADD MICRO FEEDBACK WHEN TEXT FULLY APPEARS
==================================================

When the thought text fully fades in:

- add a very soft glow bloom (quick, subtle)
- then fade back to normal

This creates a tiny emotional “arrival” moment.

==================================================
5. DELAY POST-REVEAL ACTIONS
==================================================

Buttons:
- ❤️ That helped
- 💭 I relate
- 🔁 Another thought

Should:
- appear after a short delay (1–2 seconds)
- fade in gently

Do NOT:
- show instantly
- interrupt reading moment

==================================================
6. OPTIONAL: SUBTLE SHIMMER BEFORE TAP
==================================================

Before user taps the bubble:

- add a very faint shimmer/light pass across the bubble
- slow and subtle

Purpose:
Hint that something is inside without being obvious

==================================================
7. KEEP EVERYTHING CALM
==================================================

Ensure:
- no fast animations
- no sharp transitions
- no loud visuals

Everything should feel:
- soft
- slow
- reflective

==================================================
FINAL RESULT
==================================================

The experience should feel like:

"I tapped something, and a real thought appeared from someone else... and for a moment, I paused."

The user should:
- feel a small connection
- stay on screen for a few seconds
- consider pressing receive again later
